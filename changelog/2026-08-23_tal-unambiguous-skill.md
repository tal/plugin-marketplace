# Add `unambiguous` writing skill

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Scope:** `plugins/tal/skills/unambiguous/SKILL.md`, plugin and marketplace READMEs

## What changed

Added an `unambiguous` skill to the `tal` plugin. Give the workflow a document and it makes references and grammatical structures resolve to one natural reading while preserving the original meaning and voice. The skill keeps already-clear pronouns, makes the smallest necessary edits, reports material ambiguities, and avoids guessing when context cannot establish the intended meaning.

The skill's frontmatter describes the user requests that should trigger automatic invocation, including clarifying a document, removing ambiguity, fixing unclear pronouns or antecedents, and disambiguating a sentence.
